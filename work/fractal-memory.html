Introduction

Live Sketchbook at Arte Kids Park brings visitors’ drawings into a shared digital environment. A character coloured on paper appears on the media wall. My work connected image processing, character movement and spatial visuals to support this experience.

Responsibilities

Developed webcam image capture, storage and transfer, with OpenCV processing to generate character textures.

Built levels and shaders using 3D assets provided by the design team.

Developed character paths, population tests and day-to-night transitions.

Tested multi-projector output, synchronisation, blending and mapping.
