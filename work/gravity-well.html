Gravity Well

2022

An interactive installation project responding to the audience's movement. Gravity data is converted into visual and auditory elements to deepen the sense of immersion throughout the space.

Role

Built the interaction environment in Unreal Engine

Integrated ZigSim sensor data

Collaborated on physics simulation and sound design
