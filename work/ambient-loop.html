Motion Blur

2022

A media art curation project that presents environmental data in a circular structure. Multiple channels of video loop together to form a single spatial narrative.

Role

  • Built a multi-channel video scheduling system
  • Developed a Unity-based playback and sync environment
  • Collaborated on sound and video placement in the exhibition space
