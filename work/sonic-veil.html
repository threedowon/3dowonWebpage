Sonic Veil

Year2024
TypeInteractive
MediumInteractive
TechnologyMax/MSP, TouchDesigner
ProductionSolo

An installation combining spatial audio and projection. The experience was designed so that the density of sound and texture of light shift depending on the audience's position.

Role

Developed spatial audio patches in Max/MSP

Integrated visuals in TouchDesigner
